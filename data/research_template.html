  • Pet food Small but fast-growing, import-heavy and cat-led. Locally made cat food is new (Chonk from 2024, Oskies), so a local brand can compete on price against imports that pay 25% customs duty.
  • Silage A much bigger volume market: 2.5 crore cattle, a chronic green-fodder shortage and rising feed costs. Buyers are price-sensitive dairy and fattening farmers concentrated in the Sirajganj–Pabna milk shed and the north.
  • Analysis The two products share licensing (both are "animal feed" under the 2010 Act) but not customers or channels. Pet food sells through city pet shops and vets; silage sells by the truckload to farms. Plan them as two sales teams under one feed licence.
  • Analysis A practical start: pet food in Dhaka and Chattogram (where most pet shops are), and a silage plant between the maize belt (Rangpur division, Chuadanga) and the milk shed (Sirajganj, Pabna, Bogura).

Cat food imports by origin, 2024-25

Tonnes; 32,156 t total. TBS News
  • Size: ৳236 crore pet market growing ~13% a year (survey); pet food and accessories about ৳200 crore, growing 20%+ (Financial Express). The cat food market alone is put at ৳500 crore, ৳400 crore of it imported (TBS). Estimates differ by method; treat them as a range.
  • Customers: 90% of pets are cats. Pet ownership rose about 170% in three years. Owners spend ৳1,000–3,000 a month per animal before vet bills.
  • Imports: food mainly from Thailand, Turkey and China. Survey shares: Thailand 42%, Turkey 31%, China 12%. Prices follow the taka and freight costs; importers reported 15–40% cost rises after 2022.
  • Shelf brands: budget Whiskas, Me-O, SmartHeart; mid-range Reflex, Drools, Kaniva; premium Royal Canin. Local: Chonk dry cat food at about ৳332/kg, Oskies.
  • Analysis A local dry cat food priced between budget imports and Chonk, sold through the pet shops and vets in the directory, is the clearest gap. Dog food is a small niche (10% of pets).

Silage Market

Milk: production still below demand

Lakh tonnes, 2015-16 to 2024-25; demand is 2024-25 at 250 ml/person/day. DLS
  • Herd (2024-25): 251.71 lakh cattle, 15.32 lakh buffalo, 272.91 lakh goats, 39.81 lakh sheep. Livestock is 16.5% of agricultural GDP (৳91,036 crore, current prices).
  • Milk: 155.38 lakh tonnes produced vs 162.33 lakh tonnes demand; 239 ml per person per day vs a 250 ml target. Output has doubled since 2015-16, so herds and feed demand keep growing.
  • Fodder gap: green grass meets only about one third of cattle feed needs (Financial Express via Benison). Studies put the national deficit at 40%+ of dry matter, 65% of crude protein and 60% of energy (feeds & fodder study).
  • Farm economics (2022): milk cost ৳48–50/kg vs selling price ৳41–50; a 17-cow farm spent ৳23–25k of ৳28–30k a week on feed. Paddy straw rose to ৳500/maund and Napier grass doubled (Daily Star). The country needs about 80 lakh tonnes of animal feed a year.
  • Milk shed: Pabna and Sirajganj produce about 28 lakh litres a day from 16.28 lakh cattle, roughly half of the country's milk. PRAN works with 12,000 dairy farmers in Rangpur, Natore, Pabna and Sirajganj.
  • Seasons: green fodder is scarcest in the monsoon and floods; fattening for Eid-ul-Azha runs in the months before Eid, when about half of the year's cattle are slaughtered. Analysis Silage stored from the maize harvest sells best in both windows.
  • Raw material: maize area hit a record 6.72 lakh ha in 2024-25 (~5.7–6 million t). About 80% comes from Rangpur division, Chuadanga and a few nearby areas (Daily Star). Poultry and fish feed mills compete for the same maize.
  • Price: maize silage is commonly quoted around ৳12/kg (৳600 per 50 kg bag), but we couldn't confirm this from a primary source. Get quotes from the producers below before pricing.

Licences & import duties Both

  • Law: the Fish Feed and Animal Feed Act 2010 and the Animal Feed Rules 2013 cover processing, quality, import, export, sale and transport of animal feed (Act, USDA summary of the Rules).
  • Licences: separate licences are needed to run a feed factory, to import or export feed and ingredients, and to sell feed. Rules set nutrition standards by animal type and age. Apply through the Department of Livestock Services (Trade Portal procedure).
  • Customs duty (CD) on imports (NBR tariff, chapter 23): dog or cat food for retail sale (2309.10.00) 25%; vitamin/mineral/amino-acid premixes, probiotics for feed (2309.90.11–19) 5%; other feed preparations (2309.90.90) 10%; vegetable fodder materials (2308.00.00) 0%. VAT, advance tax and other charges come on top; check the full total with a customs agent.
  • Analysis Finished imported pet food pays 25% duty while premix inputs pay 5%, which favours making pet food locally from imported premixes.
  • Other approvals to check: BSTI standards and labelling for packaged products, factory trade licence, environmental clearance for a silage plant.

What to find out next

Public data stops here. These answers need calls and field visits:

  1. Pet shop margins and terms: call 20–30 shops from the directory (Dhaka, Chattogram, Bogura, Sylhet) to learn the margin they expect, credit terms, best-selling pack sizes and price points.
  2. Silage price and willingness to pay: get quotes from Khan's Silage, Agrow and small producers; visit 15–20 dairy farms in Sirajganj and Pabna to ask what they pay for straw, grass and concentrate, and what they'd pay for silage delivered.
  3. Maize sourcing: price green maize per acre from growers in Rangpur division and Chuadanga at harvest, and transport cost to the milk shed.
  4. Licences: confirm the feed-factory, import and sales licences, fees and timelines with the District Livestock Office where the plant will be.
  5. Import cost: have a clearing agent calculate the full landed cost (duty, VAT, advance tax) for pet food premixes, packaging and finished pet food.
  6. Silage handling: test shelf life after opening, bag vs bale packaging, and a monsoon storage plan before scaling.
