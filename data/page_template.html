A starting map for expansion: the market's size, what's known in each of the 64 districts, the biggest companies, and a searchable list of every business collected.

Read counts as "known", not "total". This list combines Google Maps (when collected), Facebook business pages (via the free Overture Maps dataset), Posha Ghor, OpenStreetMap and Chonk's list of shops that sell its food, with duplicates removed. Reports estimate 4,000+ pet shops nationwide, and many small shops have no online presence, so check a district locally before deciding.
